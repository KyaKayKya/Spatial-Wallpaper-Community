# Sources and licenses

## Approved visual reference
The user-supplied approved image `1000073667.png` was used for composition, materials and lighting direction. It is not used as a scene texture. The editable scene is modeled geometry, not a projection of the reference.

## Poly Haven: Interior Tiles
- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/interior_tiles
- Download used: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/interior_tiles/interior_tiles_diff_2k.jpg
- License: CC0 1.0. https://polyhaven.com/license
- Reuse: a grout-free ceramic interior crop supplies subtle surface wear in the custom orthogonal square-tile base-color and roughness maps. The diagonal tile arrangement from the source was not used.
- The source file and crop are included for transparent provenance. They may be freely reused under CC0.

## Original work
The room, column, staircase, ring, tile grid maps, ring print, UVs, modeling/export scripts and lighting setup were created for this scene. No paid asset, restricted asset-library raw file, third-party furniture model, or proprietary water plugin is included.

## Intel Open Image Denoise
- Official download: https://www.openimagedenoise.org/downloads.html
- Official release: https://github.com/OpenImageDenoise/oidn/releases/tag/v2.5.1
- Used locally for HDR denoising with albedo and normal guides. Its binaries are excluded from the package. See the official project for its Apache 2.0 license and notices.
